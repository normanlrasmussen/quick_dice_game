2 Players roll dice aganist eachother. 

Player 1: They roll 2 dice until;
1) They bust by rolling higher than 21 - Stop and lose
2) They hit 21 and win -> Stop and win
3) They choose to stand -> Continue


Player 2: They roll 2 dice until
1) They bust by rolling higher than 21 - Lose
2) They roll the same as player 1 -> Tie
3) They roll higher than player 1 -> Win
