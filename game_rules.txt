2 Players roll dice aganist eachother. 

Player 1: They roll 2 dice until;
1) They choose to stand -> Continue
2) They hit 21 and win -> Stop and win
3) They bust by rolling higher than 22 - Stop and lose

Player 2: They roll 2 dice until
1) They bust by rolling higher than 22 - Lose
2) They roll higher than player 1 -> Win
3) They roll the same as player 1 -> Tie
